## How to prepare

Behavioral rounds fail on retrieval, not on content. You have the stories; the
problem is producing the right one in ten seconds under pressure.

1. **Build a story bank first.** Six to eight stories total. Each should be
   reusable across several question types — a good conflict story is also a
   leadership story and a communication story.
2. **Write each in STAR** — Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep Action longest
   and always land a quantified Result.
3. **Index by theme**, not by question: conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity,
   influence without authority, tight deadline, disagreement with a manager.
4. **Rehearse out loud, timed.** Two minutes per story. Written answers that
   sound fine on the page fall apart when spoken.
5. **Prepare the reverse interview.** Pick three questions from
   `reverse-interview/` per company and make them specific to that team.
